# Support Team Onboarding Guide

New support engineers complete the Helios ticketing certification during their first week.

## First week
- Shadow two senior engineers on live tickets.
- Complete the Helios ticketing certification (about three hours).
- Read the escalation runbook in docs/escalation-runbook.md.

## Access
Access to customer data is granted only after the certification is complete and
your manager approves the request in the access portal.
